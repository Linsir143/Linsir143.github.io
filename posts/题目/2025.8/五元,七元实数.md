对实数$a,b,c,d,e$，有
$$
5\sum_{\text{cyc}} (a - b)^2 \sum_{\text{cyc}} (a - c)^2 \geq \left( \sum_{\text{sym}} (a - b)^2 \right)^2
$$
右边平方内有 $\dbinom{5}{2} = 10$项.

证明：
$$LHS - RHS = \sum_{\text{cyc}} \left( b^2 - ac - bc + ad + cd - d^2 - be + ce \right)^2$$
一个取等条件为
$$
\left\{a=c-\frac{1}{2} \left(1+\sqrt{5}\right) (-1+d),b=\frac{1}{2} \left(2+\left(1+\sqrt{5}\right) c-\left(1+\sqrt{5}\right) d\right),e=1\right\}
$$
